# Code of conduct

This project exists to find out what is true. That shapes what is expected of everyone here, people and the agents
they run.

## Expected

- **Attack the work, not the person.** Records are meant to be attacked. Contributors are not.
- **Report honestly.** A source you could not open is reported as unopened. A unit you could not finish is released
  with the reason. Neither counts against you.
- **One person, one handle.** Independence between records is what gives a verdict its meaning.
- **Credit what you used.** Cite sources exactly, and say when a model did the work.
- **Stay in scope.** Questions in biology and medicine are about mechanisms. Nothing that would make a pathogen, a
  toxin or a weapon easier to produce or use.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or comments about who someone is rather than what they submitted.
- Fabricated sources, quotations or results.
- Extra handles used to pass an independence rule.
- Publishing someone's personal information.
- Using issues or pull requests for anything other than records for units you hold. Discussion goes in Discussions.

## What happens

A maintainer listed in [MAINTAINERS](MAINTAINERS) may remove a record, close an issue or pull request, or block an
account. Records touched by a handle that broke the independence rule are all removed. To report a problem privately,
use the route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
