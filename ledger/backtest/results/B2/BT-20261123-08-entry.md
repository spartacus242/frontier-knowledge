Question (headline): Can a handful of transcription factors reprogram a fully differentiated adult cell back into an embryonic-like pluripotent cell?

Question (canonical): Does there exist a combination of at most six factors drawn from a pre-declared pool P of 14 ES-enriched, pluripotency-implicated mouse genes whose continuous forced expression in cultured adult mouse somatic cells carrying a clonal, lineage-irreversible genomic mark (rearranged Igh/Tcrb) yields at least one clonal line that reactivates the endogenous Oct4/Nanog loci with the donor programme silenced, self-renews indefinitely, forms three-germ-layer teratomas and contributes to chimaeras with germline transmission, while PCR-positive for the donor rearrangement — YES on one such line; NO when every deliverable subset of P of size ≤ 6 has been expressed and assayed under Oct4/Nanog-locus selection with no clone meeting all criteria and the failure point named?
