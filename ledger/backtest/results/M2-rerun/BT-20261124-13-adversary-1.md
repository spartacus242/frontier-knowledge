# BT-20261124-13 - Gate 4, adversary #1 (BACKTEST, as-of 1978-09-01)

## Restatement (my words)

HEADLINE: Is there a connection between the dimensions of the irreducible representations of the (still conjectural) largest sporadic simple group and the Fourier coefficients of a classical modular function such as j?

OPERATIONAL TEST (v2, repair included): Assume the Fischer-Griess group M exists and has a smallest non-trivial irreducible character chi of degree 196883. Write j - 744 = q^-1 + sum c(n) q^n. Can one choose, for every n >= 1, an honest (non-virtual) representation of M of dimension c(n), the first being trivial + chi, so that for every element g the series q^-1 + sum (trace of g on the n-th representation) q^n is a weight-zero modular function, holomorphic on the upper half-plane, for some Gamma_0(N) whose level N involves only primes dividing the order of g? "Yes" = such a sequence exhibited and proved; "no" = (as worded) a proof that for at least one class no admissible sequence works.

(d) LEMMA USED BY THE RECORD, PROVED HERE. For coprime M, N the groups Gamma_0(M) and Gamma_0(N) generate SL_2(Z): both contain [[1,1],[0,1]]; they contain [[1,0],[M,1]] and [[1,0],[N,1]], hence [[1,0],[aM+bN,1]] = [[1,0],[1,1]]; the two elementary matrices generate SL_2(Z). So the record's claim that v2 kills the two-character family is correct. It extends: if for some g0 of p-power order the functional "value at g0 minus value at 1", restricted to the span W of the H_n, is a combination of the same functionals at elements of p'-power order (p' != p), then T_g0 - T_1 is invariant under SL_2(Z), holomorphic on H and vanishes at infinity, so it is 0 and H_1(g0) = 196884, contradicting H_1 = 1 + chi.

(e) THE CONSTRUCTION THAT P10(iii) ASKED FOR. Over GENERALIZED characters, v2 is satisfied for EVERY finite group G and EVERY character chi of degree c(1) - 1, by a construction that uses only power maps:
 - For a set of primes pi put chi_pi(g) = chi(g_pi), g_pi the pi-part of g. chi_pi is a generalized character: on a Brauer elementary (nilpotent) subgroup E = E_pi x E_pi', g -> g_pi is the projection homomorphism, so chi_pi restricted to E is a genuine character of E, and Brauer's characterization of characters applies. (Brauer's theorem is recalled, not opened.)
 - List the primes dividing |G| as p_1, ..., p_r, put pi_i = {p_i, ..., p_r}, X_i = chi_(pi_i), X_(r+1) = chi(1).1. Then X_1 = chi, and X_i(g) - X_(i+1)(g) = 0 unless p_i divides the order of g.
 - For any prime p let k_p = (eta(p tau)/eta(tau))^24 . j^(p-2) = q + O(q^2): weight zero on Gamma_0(p), holomorphic on H (Delta has no zero in H), positive integer coefficients. (For p = 2, 3, 5, 7, 13 the slower-growing h_p of (c) also serves.)
 - Define H_n = c(n).1 + sum_i k_(p_i)(n) (X_i - X_(i+1)). Then H_n is a generalized character, H_n(1) = c(n), H_1 = 196884 + chi - 196883 = 1 + chi, and T_g = (j - 744) + sum over p_i dividing ord(g) of (X_i(g) - X_(i+1)(g)) k_(p_i) is weight zero and holomorphic on H on Gamma_0(product of the primes dividing ord(g)); T_1 = j - 744.
 So every clause of v2 EXCEPT P8 (genuineness) is met with no property of M used beyond chi(1) = 196883, and j could be replaced by any q-series with the same first coefficient. ALL of the M-specific content of v2 therefore sits in the positivity clause P8.
 - Whether this family (or a variant with other choices of k) is GENUINE for M I could not test: it needs the multiplicities of the X_i - X_(i+1) in the irreducibles of M, i.e. a character table with power maps, and nothing of that kind dated before 1978-09-01 was opened by anyone (P4). Two toy instances I computed, on positivity of the non-trivial constituents only: for S_3 with chi the 2-dimensional character the family with k_2 = h_2, k_3 = h_3 is GENUINE for all 1 <= n <= 398; for A_5 with chi of degree 4 or 5 and k_p = h_p it FAILS already at n = 2 (multiplicity -12 on an irreducible). So genuineness is neither automatic nor impossible. UNVERIFIED for M; this kills nothing.
