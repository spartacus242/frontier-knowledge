Question (headline): Are the dimensions of the representations of the largest sporadic simple group related to the coefficients of a classical modular function?
Question (canonical): Granting that the Fischer-Griess group M exists with an irreducible character of degree 196883, is there a sequence of M-modules V_1, V_2, ... with dim V_n = c(n) for every n >= 1 (j = 1/q + 744 + sum c(n) q^n) and V_1 isomorphic to trivial + the degree-196883 irreducible, such that for every g in M the series 1/q + sum tr(g|V_n) q^n is a modular function for a discrete group commensurable with the modular group - YES on a construction or existence proof, NO on a proof that some class admits none, VOID if M or the character does not exist?
Reformulation note: this is the Gate 1 reformulation (the one permitted). The wording as supplied lacked the V_1 clause and was vacuous (V_n = c(n) trivial modules gives j - 744 for every g; record's own reasoning). The repair is itself trivially satisfiable (attack 1, both adversaries), and no second reformulation is allowed.
Alternative formulations: (1) Is there an infinite-dimensional graded representation of M with graded dimension j - 744, degree-1 piece 1 + 196883, and a modular graded character at every element? Both are met by the same construction.
