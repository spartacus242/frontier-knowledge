Step 1 - B is a modular function for Gamma_0(2). For gamma = (a b; c d) in SL_2(Z) with c even, 2*gamma(tau) = gamma'(2 tau) with gamma' = (a 2b; c/2 d) in SL_2(Z), and (c/2)(2 tau)+d = c tau + d. Since Delta has weight 12 for SL_2(Z), Delta(2 gamma tau) = (c tau+d)^12 Delta(2 tau) and Delta(gamma tau) = (c tau+d)^12 Delta(tau), so B(gamma tau) = B(tau). B is holomorphic on the upper half-plane (Delta has no zero there). Gamma_0(2) has two cusps: at infinity B = q + ...; at 0, B(-1/tau) = 2^-12 Delta(tau/2)/Delta(tau) = 2^-12 q^(-1/2)(1 + ...), a pole of finite order. Step 2 - a_n := c(n) - 196883 b_n is a non-negative integer for every n >= 1, and a_1 = 1. Let P = prod(1-q^m)^-24 = sum p(m) q^m (p(m) = number of 24-coloured partitions of m: 1, 24, 324, 3200, ...) and E_4^3 = sum e_k q^k with every e_k >= 0 and e_0..e_3 = 1, 720, 179280, 16954560.

A partition mu of m has at most 24 preimages of the "deleted a 1" kind and at most one of the other kind, because the lowered part is then the unique smallest part of mu.
