Question (headline): Can a material with a negative refractive index be built, and could it focus light more finely than the diffraction limit allows?

Question (canonical): For a passive composite of sub-wavelength conducting elements whose effective permittivity and permeability are both negative in a common frequency band, does (i) a wedge of it refract a beam to the same side of the surface normal as the incident beam, with a transmission passband appearing only where both responses are negative, and (ii) a plane-parallel slab of it form, of a source much smaller than the free-space wavelength, an image outside the slab whose intensity full width at half maximum is below half the free-space wavelength when the same source at the same source-to-detector distance with the slab removed gives a width above it - yes if both hold at any electromagnetic frequency, no if (i) fails in every such structure or if (i) holds but (ii) is excluded by measurement or by solving Maxwell's equations for the slab?
(No Gate 1 reformulation was used; the wording is the developer's, unchanged. So there is no repair clause carrying presuppositions of its own.)

Alternative formulations:
1. Solve Maxwell's equations for a point dipole in front of a homogeneous slab with relative permittivity and permeability both equal to minus one plus a small declared loss; do Fourier components with transverse wavenumber above the free-space wavenumber reach the image plane with larger amplitude than after the same path in vacuum, and does any buildable periodic structure of wires and resonant loops reproduce that slab closely enough that the measured image width falls below half a wavelength?
2. Does a manufacturable medium exist in which phase velocity and energy flow are antiparallel over an isotropic band (shown by negative-angle Snell refraction through a prism), and does a flat lens cut from it resolve two mutually incoherent point sources closer than half the free-space wavelength at an image plane where they are unresolved without the lens?
