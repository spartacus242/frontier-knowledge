## 1. Restatement, in my own words

**Headline layer (as I read it).** Is the host's own anti-tumour T-cell response held back by a brake sitting on the T cell itself, so that merely occupying that one receptor with an antibody — adding no vaccine, no cytokine, no transferred cells — lets the host destroy a tumour that is already growing?

**Operational layer (version 2, the one permitted Gate 1 repair, which is the wording I attack).** Take immunocompetent syngeneic mice carrying palpable tumours of ≥5 mm mean diameter of a named transplantable line that a pre-run arm of the same colony has qualified as poorly immunogenic (≤10% spontaneous rejection and ≤10% rejection after immunisation with irradiated cells of that line). After that size is reached, give systemic anti-CTLA-4 monoclonal antibody or an isotype-matched control. Does ≥30% of the treated group — against ≤5% of controls — go to complete regression with no palpable tumour for ≥60 days, with the effect abolished by combined CD4/CD8 depletion, with survivors resisting rechallenge, and with receptor occupancy documented on T cells including draining lymphoid tissue? Mid-range clearance (10–29%) is pre-declared PARTIAL; death from autoimmunity before the tumour endpoint is pre-declared UNINTERPRETABLE.

I also note what version 1 and version 2 share: the headline's "strong enough to reject established tumours" is operationalised as a *fraction of animals cleared and staying clear*, not as growth delay.

## 7. Recommended verdict

**Provisional.** Nothing is Unresolved, so attacks 1, 2 and 4 do not reject it; attack 4 carries a **CONTESTED** clause (CTLA-4's sign of effect, sourced both ways in pre-as-of print and already flagged at Gate 1), which caps the entry at Provisional; and novelty does not hold on both layers, because the headline layer stays **Unverified** after a second reader failed to find any route to the body of Allison, Hurwitz & Leach 1995. **Promotion conditions, in order:** (1) the body of Curr Opin Immunol 1995;7(5):682–686 — if it raises inhibitory-receptor or CTLA-4 blockade, the headline is ASKED and the entry is Rejected and routed; (2) the body of Awwad & North 1988, which would show whether brake removal cleared a tumour in a **non-thymectomized** host and at what size, and could convert my attack 2 from Partial to fatal; (3) Kearney et al. 1995, still unopened after two readers.

**Nominations, justified by kind.** For (1) the needed document kind is the review's **own body** — a document covering tumour-immunotherapy strategy that **prints** either a mention of inhibitory-receptor/CTLA-4 blockade or an enumeration of strategies that excludes it; the nominee **is** that kind (I have its front matter and abstract from the Pure record), and the obstacle is purely access: print-era Elsevier, no eprint route in 9 searches across two logs. For (2) the needed kind is a **primary experiment** that varies host thymic status and tumour size and prints a per-animal regression fraction; S1 is that kind (its abstract names the dose, the host and the outcome), and I know it because I read its abstract at the publisher — the body is gated, not absent. For (3) the needed kind is a **primary in-vivo study** that administers anti-CTLA-4 systemically and prints a response magnitude; Kearney 1995 is that kind on its title, which is a *title-level* judgement and I mark it as such — J Immunol 1995 was never fetched by either reader, so no one has verified that it administered antibody in vivo at all.

**Single strongest killing point.** Gate 2's nearest-prior set omits the pre-1996 suppressor-T-cell programme, which is the nearest prior formulation of the **headline** and in which the headline was asked and answered YES in print — Awwad & North, J Exp Med 1988;168(6):2193–2206 (DOI 10.1084/jem.168.6.2193), whose abstract states that "removal of tumor-induced L3T4+ suppressor T cells results in the release of Lyt-2+ effector T cells from suppression", with "complete regression of this tumor and in long-term host survival" — so the record owes a rule-6 transfer argument for the one prior that most needs one and never wrote it. It survives as Partial, not fatal, only because that prior's host was **thymectomized**, its intervention **depleted a cell population** rather than blocking a receptor on the responding cell, no regression fraction is printed anywhere I could read, and my read level is **abstract-only from one laboratory**.

**Which leg my verdict would stand on if the others fell.** Attack 4's CONTESTED clause 3, which rests on one full text I read myself (S2) and on the record's own Gate 1 flag. If attack 2's North line were withdrawn entirely, and attack 6 and 7 with it, the verdict would still be Provisional on clause 3 plus the Unverified headline layer.
