## Restatement (my words, both layers)

HEADLINE: when neural networks get more training data and more parameters, does their test error come down along one simple, forecastable curve family - a floor plus a power of the data plus a power of the model size - whatever the task?

OPERATIONAL TEST: train from scratch on nested subsets 2^0 .. 2^-7 of four public sets (ImageNet-1k; One Billion Word; WMT English-German; LibriSpeech 960 h), at five widths of one fixed-depth family per task, one random search per cell, three seeds. Fit c + a*n^-alpha + b*m^-beta to the 15 cells with n <= 2^-3 and the three smallest widths. YES only if, on all four tasks, that surface predicts the other 25 cells with median relative error <= 10% AND with lower held-out error than a logarithmic surface and an exponential surface. Anything else is NO, with the failing task and the better form named.

There was no Gate 1 reformulation, so there is no repair clause to test.
