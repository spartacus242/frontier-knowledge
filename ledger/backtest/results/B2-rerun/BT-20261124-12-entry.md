Question (canonical): Take as donor a clonally derived cell that is terminally differentiated by a pre-declared criterion and carries a pre-declared heritable mark of somatic origin (an irreversible somatic rearrangement such as a rearranged Ig or TCR locus, or a label driven by a terminal-differentiation promoter); force-express, by stable integration, combinations of at most ten sequence-specific transcription factors drawn from a rule-generated list (every such factor reported in pre-2001 print as enriched in, or required by, pluripotent embryonic or germline-derived cells relative to differentiated somatic tissue), singly and in combination; does any clone arising from such a donor satisfy all five of (a) self-renewal beyond ~20 passages, (b) the donor somatic mark, (c) transcription of the endogenous pluripotency loci from their own promoters with the transgenes dispensable, (d) loss of the donor differentiation programme, (e) ectoderm, mesoderm and endoderm derivatives in a teratoma and chimaera contribution including germ line? One such clone counts as YES; failure of every tested combination, with combinations, expression levels and clone counts printed, counts as a NO bounded by exactly that list.
