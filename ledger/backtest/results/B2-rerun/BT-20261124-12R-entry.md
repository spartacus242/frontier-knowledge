Question (headline): Can a handful of transcription factors reprogram a fully differentiated adult cell back into an embryonic-like pluripotent cell? (carried verbatim from the ranker, 19 words)
Question (canonical): Drawing only on the enumerated twelve-member candidate set {Oct4/Pou5f1, Sox2, Rex1/Zfp42, FoxD3/Genesis, UTF1, Hesx1/Rpx, Gbx2, Esrrb, Cdx2, Eomes, c-Myc, constitutively active Stat3}, does at least one of the 2,509 subsets of size one to six, delivered by coding-sequence-only viral or episomal vector into an intact adult donor cell meeting all three declared terminal-differentiation criteria (lineage-restricted mature phenotype; zero colony formation by the matched empty-vector arm; an irreversible clonal genomic mark of maturity, the worked instance being a completed V(D)J rearrangement in a mature peripheral-blood lymphocyte, which doubles as the donor-identity tag), with the nucleus never leaving its own cytoplasm and with oocyte/egg cytoplasm, nuclear transfer, cytoplast transfer, fusion, hybrids, heterokaryons, fusion-permitting co-culture and egg/ES extracts prohibited at every step, yield a clone that is serially passaged ≥20 continuous passages in standard ES/LIF conditions with undiminished clonogenicity and normal karyotype, transcribes endogenous Oct4 and Rex1 on a vector-discriminating assay at bona-fide-ES levels, extinguishes the donor lineage programme, carries the donor's sequenced clonal mark, and contributes to all three germ layers plus the germ line in a chimaera — YES on one such clone against zero from an equal-sized empty-vector arm with per-arm clone counts, NO only if all 2,509 subsets, each on ≥10^6 qualifying donor cells, yield nothing past partial dedifferentiation?
Reformulation note: this is the Gate 1 reformulation (the one permitted), pinning "indefinite self-renewal" to ≥20 passages/≈50 doublings and "endogenous pluripotency loci" to vector-discriminated endogenous Oct4 and Rex1, plus a coding-sequence-only vector constraint; both versions are in batches/backtest-B2-r2/BT-20261124-12R-record.md.
Alternative formulations: (1) With egg, nuclear transfer and fusion off the table, can forced expression of ≤6 named embryo-enriched transcription factors turn a mature lymphocyte carrying its own rearranged antigen-receptor locus into a cell that founds all three germ layers and the germ line, at a rate the empty-vector arm does not produce? (2) Is the pluripotent state reachable from a terminally differentiated adult nucleus left in its own cytoplasm by transcription factors alone, and if so what is the smallest sufficient subset of the twelve?
Motivating gap: Pre-as-of work brackets the question without answering it. Oocyte-dependent reprogramming (Wilmut 1997; Wakayama 1998) establishes nuclear competence, not sufficiency; pluripotent×somatic hybrids (Miller & Ruddle 1976; Tada 1997) show a whole pluripotent partner can dominate a somatic nucleus without isolating the agents; MyoD-type single-factor conversion (Davis 1987) stays inside the soma; Niwa 2000's dose-dependence is a specific reason to doubt a single-factor route.
