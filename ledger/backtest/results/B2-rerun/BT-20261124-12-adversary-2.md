## Restatement, in my own words

**Headline layer (as carried from the ranker):** can forcing a few embryonic transcription factors into a fully differentiated adult cell turn that cell back into an embryonic-like pluripotent cell?

**Operational test (version 2, the Gate 1 repair, which is the wording I attack):** start from a clonally derived adult cell that is terminally differentiated "by a pre-declared criterion" and carries a heritable mark of somatic origin — either an irreversible V(D)J-type rearrangement in a mature lymphocyte, or a label installed under a terminal-differentiation promoter in a non-lymphoid cell. Stably integrate combinations of **at most ten** sequence-specific transcription factors drawn from a rule-generated list (every TF reported in print before 2001-01-01 as enriched in or required by pluripotent embryonic/germline cells relative to differentiated somatic tissue), singly and combinatorially. Ask whether any resulting clone satisfies all five of: ~20+ passages self-renewal; the donor somatic mark; endogenous pluripotency loci transcribed from their own promoters with the transgenes dispensable; loss of the donor differentiation programme; and three germ layers in a teratoma **plus** germ-line chimaera contribution. One such clone = YES. Failure of every combination actually tested, with combinations, expression levels and clone counts printed = a NO bounded by exactly that list.
