## Restatement, in my own words

**Headline layer (as the ranker fixed it, restated):** is the differentiated state of an adult cell held in place by a short list of trans-acting regulators — so that forcing a few embryonic transcription factors into such a cell turns it into something equivalent to an embryonic pluripotent cell?

**Operational layer (version 2, restated):** start from a single-cell-derived adult cell that (a) meets a pre-declared criterion of terminal differentiation and (b) carries a heritable stamp proving somatic origin — either an irreversible antigen-receptor rearrangement, or a label installed off a terminal-differentiation promoter. Stably integrate combinations of **at most ten** sequence-specific transcription factors, drawn not from an enumerated list but from a **rule** ("every sequence-specific DNA-binding TF reported in print before 2001-01-01 as enriched in, or required by, pluripotent embryonic or germline-derived cells relative to differentiated somatic tissue"), singly and in combination. One clone that shows >~20-passage self-renewal, the donor stamp, endogenous pluripotency-locus transcription with the transgenes dispensable, loss of the donor programme, and three-germ-layer plus germ-line contribution = YES. Universal failure = a NO bounded by the combinations actually run.

So: two layers, and the second is a one-sided existence screen over a combinatorial space generated by a date-indexed literature rule.
