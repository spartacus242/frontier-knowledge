## Restatement (my words)

HEADLINE: Do bacteria infecting the stomach cause long-standing inflammation of the stomach lining and ulcers of the stomach and duodenum?

OPERATIONAL TEST (canonical): a two-limb conjunction on adult patients.
Limb 1 (association): among adults having upper endoscopy with an antral biopsy, is some bacterium living on the mucosa (seen on histology and/or grown from the biopsy) found in most of those with histological chronic gastritis and in most of those with a gastric or duodenal ulcer that is not explained by anti-inflammatory drugs or a gastrin-secreting tumour, and seldom found where the mucosa is histologically normal?
Limb 2 (intervention): does getting rid of that organism with antimicrobials, compared with healing the ulcer by acid suppression alone, make the gastritis resolve and reduce endoscopically documented ulcer relapse over 12 months?
YES only if both limbs hold; NO if the organism is as common on normal mucosa, or if clearance changes neither gastritis nor relapse. No Gate 1 reformulation was made, so there is no repair clause to test separately.

## Attack 4 worked clause by clause (before the table)

MEASURAND, NOT A PRESUPPOSITION - I declare the exemption rather than grade around it: limb 1 reports presence in diseased versus histologically normal mucosa, so a contaminant would show as "as common on normal mucosa" and return NO. Attack 4 does not reach this clause.
3. ONE organism ("that organism") accounts for the association across gastritis, gastric ulcer and duodenal ulcer. NOT converted to a measurand by the wording. UNVERIFIED. The opened and described priors name different agents (streptococci in S1 at full text; spirochetes and Pseudomonas in the record's P3 and P2), which shows the premise is not established but does not show it false - different reports of different organisms are silent on whether a common one exists. Kills nothing. The record's Gate 1 note raised this clause itself.
4. An antimicrobial regimen exists that clears the organism durably, and clearance can be confirmed on repeat biopsy. UNVERIFIED; the record routes failure of this clause to an INDETERMINATE outcome, which protects against a false NO but feeds attack 5.
5. Acid suppression alone heals ulcers and does not itself clear the organism. UNVERIFIED (nothing opened).
6. Ulcer relapse after healing is frequent enough within 12 months to show a difference. UNVERIFIED by me (the record's source for it was not located).
7. Gastric ulcer, duodenal ulcer and chronic gastritis can be pooled under one cause. The YES rule pools them.
