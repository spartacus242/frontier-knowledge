## Restatement (my words)

HEADLINE: Is long-standing stomach inflammation, and the ulcers of the stomach and duodenum, the result of a bacterium living in the stomach?

OPERATIONAL TEST: Take adults coming to endoscopy, leaving out regular aspirin/anti-inflammatory users and gastrinoma. Three things are asked, each reported separately for chronic gastritis, duodenal ulcer and gastric ulcer. (i) On biopsies read blind and cultured, do mucosal bacteria turn up in clearly more diseased stomachs than histologically normal ones? (ii) Does a pure culture of them give a previously normal stomach gastritis? (iii) In a randomised trial, does an antibacterial regimen that demonstrably clears them make the gastritis resolve and cut 12-month endoscopic ulcer relapse against acid suppression alone? YES if all three, NO if no association or if clearance changes nothing, COLONISER-ONLY if only (i).
