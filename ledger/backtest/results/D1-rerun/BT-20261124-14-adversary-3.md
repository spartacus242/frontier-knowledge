## Restatement (both layers, my words)

HEADLINE: Do bacteria living in the stomach cause chronic gastritis and peptic ulcers?

OPERATIONAL TEST: Take adults coming to endoscopy who are not on aspirin-type drugs and have no gastrinoma. (i) Reading blind to diagnosis, look for bacteria in antral and body biopsies by microscopy and culture, and compare how often they are found in patients with gastritis, duodenal ulcer or gastric ulcer against patients whose mucosa is histologically normal. (ii) Give a pure culture to a host whose mucosa is normal and see whether gastritis follows. (iii) Randomise ulcer patients who carry the organism to acid suppression with or without an antibacterial regimen shown to clear it, and see whether the gastritis resolves and fewer ulcers recur within 12 months. Label the pattern YES, NO or COLONISER-ONLY.

What the record claims for it: operational novelty Medium; HEADLINE NOVELTY LOW (a paper's stated aim in 1915-16); it survives only as Under-asked through rule 5's dormant route, window 1958-01-01 to 1983-01-01.
