# Area rotation

The maintainer, 2026-10-16: the ledger was finding most of its questions in medicine or biology, and asked that the area of focus alternate each run. Measured at 65 entries, the skew was real — medicine 35 (54%), other STEM 11, biology 10, AI 9, so medicine and biology together held 69%.

So each batch draws **all** of its candidates from **one** area, and the areas rotate in a fixed cycle. **The cycle in force since 2026-11-27** is five turns: the five areas set on 2026-10-28 from the maintainer's list, with biology and medicine whole fields as they have been since 2026-11-24. The open turn for every other field, which followed medicine from 2026-11-24, was dropped when the maintainer narrowed the ledger to five fields (see "Cycle narrowed, 2026-11-27" at the foot of this file):

1. **mathematics** — pure and applied mathematics; the mathematics of computation where the object is a mathematical structure. Excludes questions whose object is a software system or a database of records (a table of mathematical objects such as OEIS or LMFDB counts as mathematics when the question is about the objects).
2. **physics** — physics and astrophysics, and the physical sciences (physical chemistry, geophysics, planetary science) where the question is about a physical mechanism. Excludes engineering of instruments and networks as such, and biology and medicine even where they use physical methods.
3. **computer science** — theory, algorithms, systems, programming languages, and AI architecture and systems (model and system design, training, evaluation, learning dynamics). Excludes applications of computing to another field, which belong to that field's batch.
4. **biology** — the whole of biology: molecules, cells, organisms, populations, ecosystems and evolution. Excludes clinical and patient-facing questions, which are medicine's. (From 2026-10-28 to 2026-11-24 this read: cell behaviour, mechanisms of disease propagation and elimination in organisms, and patterns in biological data that point to an unknown mechanism.)
5. **medicine** — the whole of medicine: disease and health in patients and populations, their mechanism, cause, course, prevention and treatment. (From 2026-10-28 to 2026-11-24 this read: cancer, mechanisms of disease propagation and elimination in patients and populations, and analyses of clinical or biological data aimed at a mechanism or a cause. It also said "Excludes audits of records, registries and reporting"; the Depth requirement excludes those in every area.)

**The open turn, retired on 2026-11-27.** From 2026-11-24 to 2026-11-27 a sixth turn, **open**, followed medicine. It took every field the five labels above leave out: the social sciences (economics, psychology, sociology, political science, anthropology, linguistics), the humanities (history, philosophy, the study of literature, music and art), law, engineering, and the parts of chemistry and of the earth and environmental sciences that the physics label does not take. It was one area made of many fields: every draft named its field, the fifteen drafts came from at least five different fields, and no more than two of the five developed candidates shared a field where the undiscarded drafts allowed it. It ran once, at 00:35 on 2026-11-26. Those fields are now out of scope, and a candidate whose target field is one of them is rejected at Gate 0 (field).

Every area is further filtered by the Depth requirement in `LEDGER.md`: a question about how a field studies its subject (methodology, records, bookkeeping) is rejected at intake whatever its area.

**The cycle from 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-23** was: 1. other STEM (physical sciences, mathematics, engineering, earth and space science), 2. AI architecture, 3. biology, 4. medicine. Its log rows below stand; it was retired after the eighth pass's first step.

Cross-field transplants are unaffected and still wanted: the method may come from anywhere, as long as the TARGET field — the one whose literature decides novelty and whose community would act on the answer — is the batch's area.

## How a run picks its area

Run `python3 -I ledger/tools/next-run.py status`. It reads the last line of the log below, takes the next area in the cycle and prints it; pass that to the batch runner as its `area` argument (the bold word of its label above), and append this batch's line when the batch is committed. A last line that names an area outside the five-turn cycle, as the open turn's line does, is followed by mathematics. A batch that fails before it commits appends nothing, so the next run takes the same area — which is correct, since that area did not get a batch.

The log is the state. Do not infer the area from entry domains or from the batch count.

## How a run picks its drafting methods

Since 2026-11-27 a run drafts four headlines, each by a different one of the fourteen generation methods in `LEDGER.md` ("Where candidates come from", rule 5). The methods are taken four at a time in the order `LEDGER.md` lists them, each run starting after the last method the run before it used and wrapping at the end of the list, so every method is used before any is used again. `python3 -I ledger/tools/next-run.py status` reads the last line of the log below and prints the next four; pass them to the batch runner as its `methods` argument, and append this batch's line when the batch is committed, the four methods separated by commas and in the order they were handed out. With no line in the log, the first run takes the first four.

The area and the methods rotate separately: five areas and fourteen methods in fours, so the same area meets different methods on each pass.

**One thing recorded here before was false, and it reached the maintainer.** The runner did NOT already enforce the cycle reading.
