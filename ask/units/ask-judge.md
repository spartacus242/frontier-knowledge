# ask-judge: Gate 5, the verdict (maintainers only)

Opens when a candidate's research record, both adversaries and every source check are merged. A maintainer weighs the
records under `LEDGER.md` Gate 5 and appends the ledger entry, crediting every contributor by handle.

```json
"status": "Validated | Provisional | Rejected",
"reason": "...",
"strongest_remaining_objection": "...",
"promotion_condition": "for Provisional: exactly what would promote it"
```
