# Research working files

These are the working files of the founding research runs: one automated run at a time picks an open problem and
works it under the rules in [../PROMPT.md](../PROMPT.md).

**Read the labels as internal labels.** CONFIRMED, PROMISING, BREAKTHROUGH and the like in `attempts.md` and
`summaries/` record how a run's own reviewers voted. They are not outside review, and nothing here has been peer
reviewed. Some entries were later downgraded or found wrong, and those stay on the record. What the project stands
behind today, and how far each claim has been checked from outside, is on [../BOARD.md](../BOARD.md) and nowhere else.

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `attempts.md` | one entry per run: the problem, what was tried, the label |
| `summaries/` | a plain-language summary per day |
| `problems/<problem>/` | programs, claim packets and reviewers' own programs for each problem |

To check a claim yourself, take its unit from the board rather than starting here: the unit states exactly what to
reproduce.
